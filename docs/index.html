Tempus

Timelapses of your screen, a single window, or your camera. Recorded and kept entirely on your Mac.

Download for Mac
Version 0.1.0 macOS 26 Tahoe or later Apple silicon and Intel 1.6 MB

Install Tempus

It takes about a minute. You don't need an account.

  1. Open the download

    Double-click Tempus.dmg in your Downloads folder. A window opens with Tempus and your Applications folder.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Drag the Tempus icon onto the Applications folder, then eject the disk image from the Finder sidebar.

  3. Open Tempus

    Find Tempus in Launchpad or your Applications folder and open it. Press ⌘N to start your first timelapse.

Allow screen or camera access

macOS asks the first time you record. Tempus only uses access while a recording is running.

Screen Recording

When you record a display or window, macOS asks to allow screen recording. If you dismissed the prompt, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on Tempus, and reopen the app.

Camera

When you pick a camera as the source, macOS asks for camera access. To change it later, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.

What it does

Two ways to record, depending on how long you'll be working.

Screenshots mode

A full-resolution frame every 5 seconds to 2 minutes. Choose which frames to keep and export an MP4 at any speed.

Video mode

A sped-up HEVC video written live, 5× to 300×. One hour at 60× plays back in one minute.

Any source

Record a whole display, one window, or a connected camera.

Crash-safe

Pause and resume whenever you like. If your Mac restarts, your recording is still there.

Save where you want

Pick an exports folder once in Settings, or choose a location each time.

Private by design

No accounts, servers, analytics, or uploads. Tempus makes no network requests.

Help

Answers to common questions.

macOS says it can't verify the developer

Official releases are notarized by Apple and open normally. If you built Tempus yourself or got a test build, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Tempus, and click Open Anyway.

Tempus doesn't see my screen after I allowed access

macOS applies Screen Recording changes when an app relaunches. Quit Tempus with ⌘Q and open it again.

Where are my recordings stored?

Inside the app's private folder on your Mac. To find them, open Tempus Settings and click Show Recordings in Finder.

Exported videos go wherever you save them, or to the exports folder you picked in Settings.

How do I update?

Download the latest version from this page and drag it to Applications, replacing the old copy. Your recordings and settings are kept.

How do I uninstall?

Drag Tempus from Applications to the Trash.

To also delete your recordings, remove ~/Library/Containers/app.tempus.timelapse. This permanently deletes every timelapse you haven't exported.
